# Release 0.11D — Client/Specialist Cabinets + Financial Lifecycle

## Support Client Cabinet

- Continuation-code access for returning clients.
- Visible credit wallet summary.
- Service-request creation and status tracking.
- Specialist linking and invitation flow.

## Health Client Cabinet

- Continuation-code access to the Health cabinet.
- Health diary and related client history.
- Specialist linking.
- Eight canonical Body services with topic and meeting-format pricing.

## Specialist Cabinet

- Specialist login with protected sessions.
- Support and Body module entitlements.
- Module-scoped client visibility.
- Service-request handling.
- Invitation, onboarding, and specialist-linking flow.

## Pricing

- Support: 7 services.
- Body: 8 services.

## Financial Lifecycle

- Reserve credits when a canonical paid request is accepted.
- Capture the reservation when a request reaches completion through a valid state progression.
- Release the reservation when a request is cancelled.
- Idempotent reserve, capture, and release operations.
- Insufficient-credit protection without partial mutations.
- Separate Support and Body wallet scopes.
- Canonical service-code and price snapshots on requests.

## Security

- Privileged financial and linking RPCs are executable by `service_role` only.
- Direct execution by `anon` and `authenticated` is denied.
- Supabase Security Advisor was checked after the security hardening rollout.

## Production Verification

- Full synthetic financial smoke passed for answered and scheduled completion paths.
- Invalid transitions and insufficient-credit behavior were verified.
- Synthetic fixtures were removed and the historical baseline was restored exactly.
- Production runtime logs were clean after verification.
- No historical requests, wallets, assignments, experts, or accounting records were altered.
