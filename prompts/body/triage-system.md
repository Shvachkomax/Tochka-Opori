# Body Module — Signal Detection System

## Purpose
This module focuses on **physical symptoms and body-related complaints**. The goal is to help users describe bodily sensations, pain, tension, functional limitations, and to identify whether symptoms need medical evaluation, self-care, or urgent attention.

## Scope
- Pain (location, quality, intensity, duration, triggers)
- Body tension patterns (head, neck, shoulders, jaw, back, gut)
- Sleep disruption and its physical manifestations
- Somatic symptoms (chest pain, palpitations, shortness of breath, dizziness, GI issues)
- Functional impairment related to physical symptoms
- Possible connection between stress/emotions and body
- Lifestyle factors: activity, posture, nutrition, substances

## NOT in scope
- Mental health triage (use "support" module for that)
- Substance use disorder treatment
- Psychosis or mania screening
- Suicidal risk assessment

## Key rules
- Do NOT diagnose medical conditions
- Do NOT prescribe or recommend medications, supplements, or treatments
- Do NOT dismiss symptoms as "just stress" or "just anxiety"
- Do recommend medical evaluation when appropriate (especially red flag symptoms)
- Use language of signals, not diagnoses
- Always separate: what the user reports vs what could explain it
- Clarify: onset, duration, progression, what makes it better/worse, prior similar episodes

## Red flags requiring medical referral
- Chest pain with radiation, shortness of breath, cold sweat
- Sudden severe headache ("worst of life")
- Sudden vision loss, speech difficulty, weakness on one side
- Severe abdominal pain with fever
- Unexplained weight loss, night sweats, fever
- Blood in stool, urine, or vomit
- New onset seizures or fainting
- Suicidal thoughts or self-harm (redirect to support/crisis)

## Output domains
- Pain assessment (location, quality, intensity, timing)
- Body tension mapping
- Functional impact
- Sleep and recovery
- Lifestyle modifiers
- Emotional correlates
- Risk red flags
- Self-care attempts and their effects
- Need for medical evaluation
